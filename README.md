# تذكّر Tadhakkar

A Flutter/Android dhikr and prayer-times app — free, works offline, no account required.

## Download

Grab the latest APK from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest).

1. On your Android phone, open the link above and tap the `Tadhakkar.apk` file under **Assets** to download it.
2. Once downloaded, open the file from your notifications or Downloads folder to start installing.
3. Android may show a **"Play Protect"** or **"Unknown sources"** warning — this is expected for apps installed outside the Play Store. Tap **Settings**, enable the toggle to allow installs from this source, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open Tadhakkar, choose your language, pick a dhikr, and start counting. Everything is saved on your device automatically.

> Tadhakkar is not yet on the Google Play Store, so installation looks a little different from most apps — but it only takes about a minute.

## Features

- Tasbeeh counter with customizable daily goals
- Adhkar (remembrance) library
- Inactivity nudges to bring you back to dhikr
- Walk Mode — counts steps as dhikr
- Works fully offline, no sign-up
